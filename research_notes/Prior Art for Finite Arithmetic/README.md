# Research notes

These are the working notes behind the [prior-art report](../../reports/Prior%20Art%20for%20Finite%20Arithmetic.md). They compare the authors' unpublished exploratory computations, called “the workbench” or “the local record” in the notes, with published literature. The literature citations are public. The workbench records themselves are not published, so statements about what the workbench contains cannot be checked from this repository.
